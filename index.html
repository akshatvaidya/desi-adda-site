FAQ

  • How do I sign in? — You can play as a guest, or sign in with Google or Apple to save your progress across devices.
  • How does multiplayer work? — Create a private room and share the code with friends, or use Quick Match to find an opponent.
  • Can I play offline? — Solo quiz mode and Heads Up work offline. Multiplayer requires an internet connection.
  • How do I earn coins? — Play quizzes, win multiplayer matches, and complete daily challenges to earn coins.
