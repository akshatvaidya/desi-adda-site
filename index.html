FAQ

  • How do I sign in? — Play as a guest, or sign in with Google or Apple to save progress across devices.
  • How does multiplayer work? — Create a private room and share the code, or use Quick Match to find opponents.
  • Can I play offline? — Solo quiz and Heads Up work offline. Multiplayer needs internet.
  • How do I earn coins? — Play quizzes, win matches, and complete daily challenges.
  • How do I delete my account? — Go to Settings → Delete Account, or email us.
